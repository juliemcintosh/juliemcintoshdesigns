I · Storytelling

Paint-on-glass, live action and paper animation

Films

Three short films, each a different way of testing how pacing, framing and movement can carry a story, often without dialogue.

2023 · Procreate, iPad

Origins of Evolution

A digital exploration of paint-on-glass animation. Working frame by frame in Procreate, I erased and repainted each layer to test how pacing and environmental movement could carry a story without dialogue.

Live action · Co-direction

Tension

A restrained exploration of psychological suspense. Set within a single room between three girls, the film examines how stillness, framing and long duration can generate unease without spectacle.

Co-directed and developed with Katie, Lucia and Maeve. My role: co-direction and visual and audio planning.

Paper animation · Created and directed

Zombie Hill

A hand-crafted paper animation exploring parody through frame-by-frame movement and layered composition.
